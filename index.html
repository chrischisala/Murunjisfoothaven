Client FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding appointments, walk-ins, and services.

Walk-ins are warmly accepted based on chair availability! However, to guarantee your preferred therapist, private suite, or specific time slot (especially on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays), we recommend booking in advance via WhatsApp or phone call.

We are conveniently situated inside Mosi-oa-Tunya Shopping and Recreation, Plot 609 Munali Road, Lusaka (right next to the Total Filling Station in Chamba Valley). The complex offers 24/7 guarded secure parking.

Yes, absolutely! Murunji’s Foot Haven is a premier unisex sanctuary. We have an Executive Gentlemen’s Barber Lounge offering fade cuts (K50), cuts & shaves (K70), beard shaves (K30), kids cuts (K40), and Caucasian cuts (K150), as well as foot reflexology and pedicure maintenance.

Hygiene is our highest priority. All metal instruments undergo ultrasonic cleansing and high-temperature autoclave sterilization before being sealed in pouches. Foot basins use fresh disposable protective liners between every single client.

We accept Airtel Mobile Money, MTN Mobile Money, local bank cards via POS, and Zambian Kwacha cash.
